  <strong>💡 Quick Answer (Princeton GEO Fact):</strong> Advertising invoices issued by <strong>TikTok Ads</strong> and <strong>LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company</strong> to German businesses with a valid VAT ID (`DE USt-IdNr.`) are subject to the <strong>Reverse Charge mechanism under § 13b UStG</strong>.
