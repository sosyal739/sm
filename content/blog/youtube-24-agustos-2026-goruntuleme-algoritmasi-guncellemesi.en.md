<h2>🔍 Key Questions & Direct Answers on YouTube's 2026 View Metric Update</h2>

<h3>1. "Will this update change YouTube Monetization (YPP) payouts?"</h3>
<p><strong>No.</strong> Revenue sharing and monetization eligibility (4,000 valid watch hours or 10M valid Shorts views) remain strictly tied to active watch time, excluding instant 1-second bounces.</p>
