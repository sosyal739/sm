<h2>🎨 The 3-Step Framework for Infinite Ad Creative Scaling</h2>

<h3>1. High-Impact 3-Second Hooks (Stop Rate Engineering)</h3>
<p>In short-form feeds, the opening frame is make-or-break. We systematically test 5 to 10 distinct visual hooks (direct problem calls, curiosity triggers, before-after contrasts) upfront while keeping high-performing body footage consistent.</p>

<h3>2. Advantage+ Generative Enhancements</h3>
<p>Meta leverages generative AI to adjust image aspect ratios dynamically, optimize contrast and brightness per user preference, and overlay dynamic product catalog highlights.</p>

<h3>3. Consolidated Account Governance</h3>
<p>We eliminate budget fragmentation by concentrating capital into high-velocity ASC architectures, enforcing strict existing customer budget caps to prioritize pure new customer acquisition (prospecting).</p>
