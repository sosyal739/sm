<h2>🎯 Why PMax Wastes Budget Without Negative Lists</h2>

<p>Performance Max serves ads across Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Maps, and Gmail. Without explicit boundaries, the bidding algorithm takes the path of least resistance:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Brand Cannibalization:</strong> Bidding heavily on your existing brand terms that would have converted organically anyway, inflating reporting numbers artificially.</li>
  <li><strong>B2C Waste on B2B Offers:</strong> Industrial manufacturers paying premium CPCs for hobbyists looking for cheap retail parts or DIY guides.</li>
  <li><strong>Low-Quality In-App Display Placements:</strong> Draining media budgets on mobile games and apps where clicks occur inadvertently.</li>
</ul>

<h2>🛠️ 3-Step Setup for PMax Negative Keywords</h2>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Account-Level Negative Lists:</strong> In <em>Tools & Settings > Shared Library > Negative Keyword Lists</em>, attach broad exclusions (jobs, salary, free, login, support) account-wide.</li>
  <li><strong>Campaign-Level Brand Exclusion:</strong> In campaign settings under <em>Brand Exclusions</em>, exclude your company name so PMax strictly drives incremental new-customer acquisition.</li>
  <li><strong>Weekly Search Terms Review:</strong> Monitor <em>Insights & Reports > Search Terms</em> weekly. Flag emerging irrelevant keywords and push them into your exclusion lists immediately.</li>
</ol>
