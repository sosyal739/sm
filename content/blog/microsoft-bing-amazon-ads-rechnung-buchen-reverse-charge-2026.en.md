  <strong>💡 Quick Answer:</strong> For German companies with a valid VAT ID (USt-IdNr.), <strong>Microsoft Advertising (Bing Ads)</strong> is invoiced net by <em>Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited</em> under the <strong>§ 13b UStG Reverse Charge mechanism</strong> (reported in German UStVA boxes 84 and 67). <strong>Amazon Ads</strong> on Amazon.de is usually billed domestically by <em>Amazon Online Germany GmbH</em> in Munich with <strong>19% German VAT</strong> — booked as normal domestic input VAT (box 66), not Reverse Charge.
</blockquote>

<div class="lead">
<p>Always verify the legal entity on the top left of your monthly PDF invoice before posting to SKR03 (<code>4600</code>) or SKR04 (<code>6600</code>) in DATEV, Lexoffice, or SevDesk. Treating a domestic 19% German VAT invoice from Amazon Online Germany GmbH as an EU Reverse Charge expense creates discrepancies in your monthly VAT return (Umsatzsteuervoranmeldung).</p>
</div>

<h2>Key Differences Between Microsoft Ads and Amazon Ads in Germany</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Microsoft Ireland Operations Limited (IE8256796U):</strong> Cross-border B2B service within the EU. Enter your German VAT ID in billing settings to receive a 0% VAT invoice marked Reverse Charge.</li>
  <li><strong>Amazon Online Germany GmbH (Munich):</strong> Domestic German entity charging 19% German MwSt. Deduct the 19% input VAT normally as Vorsteuer (Kz. 66).</li>
  <li><strong>Amazon Seller Balance Offsets:</strong> If Sponsored Products spend is deducted directly from marketplace payouts, book the gross ad invoice against your Amazon clearing account to keep marketplace revenue and ad spend cleanly separated.</li>
</ul>
