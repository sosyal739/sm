<h2>🛠️ Step-by-Step PPC Automation with Gemini 3.6</h2>

<h3>1. Automated Negative Keyword Filtering</h3>
<p>Gemini 3.6 scans account Search Term reports and automatically flags irrelevant queries that drain budget, adding them directly to campaign negative lists.</p>

<h3>2. Responsive Search Ads (RSA) Generation</h3>
<p>By mapping search intent to value propositions, Gemini 3.6 drafts high-converting headline and description combinations that maximize CTR.</p>

<h3>3. Landing Page & Ad Message Matching</h3>
<p>Gemini 3.6 compares landing page copy against ad copy, guaranteeing message match alignment that boosts Ad Rank and lowers CPC.</p>
