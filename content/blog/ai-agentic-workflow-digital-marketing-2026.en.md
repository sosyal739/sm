<h2>🤖 What is Agentic AI and How Does it Differ from Traditional Automation?</h2>
<p>Traditional marketing automation (e.g. Zapier or Make) operates on rigid "If-This-Then-That" conditional rules. In contrast, Agentic AI receives high-level strategic objectives (e.g. "Lower Cost Per Acquisition by 20%"), determines execution steps autonomously, calls external APIs, and evaluates output until goals are achieved.</p>

<p>Powered by Google Gemini 3.6 and Claude-Ads capabilities, agent networks continuously audit PPC campaigns and shift ad spend toward top-performing ad sets without requiring manual intervention.</p>
