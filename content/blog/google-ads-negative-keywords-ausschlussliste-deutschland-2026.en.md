<ul>

<h2>⚙️ How to Implement These Lists in Your Google Ads Account</h2>

<p>Follow these 3 levels of negative keyword implementation:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Account-Level Negative Keyword Lists (Recommended):</strong> Navigate to <em>Tools & Settings &gt; Shared Library &gt; Negative Keyword Lists</em> in Google Ads. Paste the universal and industry lists, name them clearly, and apply them across all current and future Search campaigns.</li>
  <li><strong>Campaign-Level Exclusions:</strong> Use campaign-specific negative lists when one campaign promotes a service that another excludes (e.g., excluding <em>"b2c"</em> from your enterprise campaign while running a consumer campaign separately).</li>
  <li><strong>Performance Max (PMax) Campaigns:</strong> PMax campaigns do not show a standard negative keywords tab. You can exclude negative terms across PMax by using the <em>Account-Level Negative Keywords</em> section or by setting up <em>Brand Exclusion Lists</em>. For advanced PMax configurations, consult our guide on <a href="/blog/google-ads-pmax-negative-keywords-kampagnen-2026">Google Ads PMax Negative Keywords</a>.</li>
