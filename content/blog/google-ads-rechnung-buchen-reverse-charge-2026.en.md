<h2>📋 4 Essential Steps for German Google Ads Bookkeeping</h2>

<p>Google issues monthly advertising invoices through Dublin (<em>Google Ireland Limited, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland</em>). Ensure your accounting department adheres to these guidelines:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>1. Register Your German VAT ID (USt-IdNr.):</strong> In Google Ads billing settings, configure your account as a business entity and supply your German VAT identification number (DE...). If set as an individual, Google charges 23% Irish VAT, which is non-recoverable in Germany.</li>
  <li><strong>2. Verify the Reverse Charge Note on PDFs:</strong> Confirm the monthly invoice explicitly states <em>„Reverse charge - Recipient is liable for VAT pursuant to Art. 196 EU VAT Directive“</em> with a 0% tax rate.</li>
  <li><strong>3. Advance VAT Return (UStVA) Entries:</strong> Declare the net expense in Field 84 (Taxable other services by an entrepreneur established in another EU member state) and offset it in Field 67 (Deductible input tax).</li>
  <li><strong>4. Credit Card Statements Do Not Suffice:</strong> In a German tax audit (Betriebsprüfung), simple credit card transaction lines are rejected as operational expense receipts. You must retain the official monthly PDF invoice.</li>
</ul>
