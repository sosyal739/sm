<h2>🧠 Understanding Reasoning AI Models</h2>
<p>Traditional Large Language Models (LLMs) focus on predicting the next token sequentially. DeepSeek-R1 and OpenAI's o3 series introduce an internal "Chain of Thought" (CoT) execution phase, self-correcting logic errors prior to producing output.</p>

<p>For SEO and digital marketing professionals, this delivers deeply researched, error-free content that matches user intent while satisfying E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) criteria.</p>
