<p><strong>Clients start on Google – “employment lawyer Cologne”, “tax advisor for start-ups”, “switch tax advisor”.</strong> If you are not visible there, competitors get the inquiry. Click prices are high in these segments, so wasted spend is expensive. This guide shows how to structure budget and keywords so clicks become clients.</p>

<h2>🔎 Which searches actually bring clients</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>High intent:</strong> “lawyer [practice area] [city]”, “tax advisor [city]”, “tax advisor for [freelancers, doctors, landlords]”, “switch tax advisor”.</li>
  <li><strong>Medium intent:</strong> “lawyer costs …”, “tax advisor cost annual accounts”, “initial consultation”. Use landing pages with price ranges and process.</li>
  <li><strong>Low intent (usually exclude):</strong> “template”, “free”, “do it yourself”, “training”, “salary”, “jobs”. Maintain them as <a href="/blog/google-ads-negative-keywords-ausschlussliste-deutschland-2026">negative keywords</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p>Our Search Console data shows demand for exactly this pattern of <em>industry + service + location</em>: queries such as “google ads für anwälte” and “seo für steuerberater” prove that firms actively look for providers.</p>

<h2>⚖️ Professional-conduct rules</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Lawyers:</strong> advertising is allowed if factual and not pushing for individual mandates (§ 43b BRAO, UWG). No misleading success promises.</li>
  <li><strong>Tax advisors:</strong> factual information is allowed, conduct-violating advertising is not (§ 57a StBerG). No guarantees such as “maximum refund”.</li>
  <li><strong>Data protection:</strong> inquiries contain sensitive data – use Consent Mode v2 and a clean form.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> orientation only, not legal advice.</p>

<h2>🏗️ Landing pages and tracking</h2>
<ol>
  <li><strong>One page per service</strong> – never send traffic to the homepage.</li>
  <li><strong>Show trust:</strong> qualifications, specialist titles, reviews, process.</li>
  <li><strong>Several contact paths:</strong> phone with call tracking, appointment booking, short form.</li>
  <li><strong>Measure conversions properly</strong> – calls, forms, bookings – ideally with <a href="/blog/server-side-gtm-meta-capi-setup-2026">server-side tracking</a>.</li>
</ol>
