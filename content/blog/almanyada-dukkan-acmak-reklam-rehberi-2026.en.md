<h2>🗺️ Step 1: Google Business Profile & Video Verification</h2>

<p>Your Google Maps profile represents your business's primary storefront online:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Select Specific Business Categories:</strong> Avoid generic terms; choose exact services (e.g., "Plumbing Contractor", "Dental Clinic", "Italian Restaurant").</li>
  <li><strong>Prepare for Video Verification:</strong> Google requires an unedited continuous recording displaying your permanent exterior signage, street name, cash register, and facility keys.</li>
  <li><strong>NAP Consistency:</strong> Ensure Name, Address, and Phone details match your official German trade register (Gewerbeanmeldung / Impressum) identically.</li>
</ul>

<h2>🎯 Step 2: Radius-Targeted Google Search Campaigns</h2>

<p>Organic ranking climbs take 2 to 4 weeks. To generate immediate revenue to cover startup overhead:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Target 10–25 km Radiuses:</strong> Restrict ad impressions strictly to consumers within convenient commuting distance.</li>
  <li><strong>Emphasize Direct Calls and Directions:</strong> Deploy click-to-call mobile extensions and direct navigation tracking.</li>
  <li><strong>Negative Keyword Protections:</strong> Eliminate terms like "salary", "job openings", or "DIY guides" to prevent wasted spend.</li>
</ul>
