<h2>⚠️ The 2 Costliest Tax Errors for Corporate Advertisers</h2>

<h3>1. Missing VAT ID Resulting in 23% Irish VAT</h3>
<p>If you fail to register a verified German USt-IdNr. before launching campaigns, Google and Meta classify the account as a consumer (B2C) and levy 23% Irish VAT. The German tax authorities (Finanzamt) will <strong>not</strong> refund foreign Irish VAT, resulting in immediate cash loss.</p>

<h3>2. Relying on Credit Card Statements</h3>
<p>Credit card slips do not qualify as legally binding tax receipts in a corporate audit. Download the official monthly VAT invoice from the billing dashboard each month.</p>
