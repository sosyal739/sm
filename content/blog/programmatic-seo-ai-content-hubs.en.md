<h2>🚀 What is Programmatic SEO (pSEO) & AI Content Hubs?</h2>
<p>Programmatic SEO merges database values (locations, categories, integrations) with dynamically engineered page templates to publish hundreds or thousands of high-intent search landing pages automatically.</p>

<p>Powered by Google Gemini 3.6 and reasoning LLMs, pSEO pages are no longer thin duplicate templates, but rich, highly customized landing pages engineered to fulfill search intent.</p>
