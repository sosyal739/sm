  <strong>💡 Quick Answer (Princeton GEO Fact):</strong> In <strong>Lexoffice</strong> and <strong>SevDesk</strong>, Google Ads and Meta Ads invoices from Ireland (0% VAT) must be booked under <strong>"Fremdleistungen § 13b" (SKR03 account 3123 / SKR04 account 5923)</strong> rather than regular "Advertising (0%)" so that boxes 84 and 67 of the German VAT return (UStVA) are populated automatically.
</blockquote>
