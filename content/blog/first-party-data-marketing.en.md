        <div class="lead">
          <p>With the deprecation of third-party cookies, digital marketing is undergoing a fundamental change. First-party data has become the only reliable way to reach target audiences.</p>
        </div>

        <h2>🍪 What is a Cookieless World?</h2>
        <p>The cookieless world refers to the era where browsers block third-party cookies that track users across sites. Apple's iOS updates and Google Chrome's cookie restrictions initiated this era.</p>

        <blockquote>
          <p>"The era of renting data from others is over; you must now own your data."</p>
        </blockquote>

        <h2>💡 Why is 1st-Party Data Worth Its Weight in Gold?</h2>
        
        <h3>1. Accuracy and Reliability</h3>
        <p>It is the data the customer gives you directly (e-mail, phone, purchase history). There is no margin for error.</p>

        <h3>2. Compliance with Privacy Laws</h3>
        <p>Since it is collected with the user's consent in accordance with data protection laws like GDPR, it carries no legal risk.</p>

        <h3>3. Competitive Advantage</h3>
        <p>This data belongs only to you and cannot be bought or used by your competitors.</p>

        <h2>📈 Necessary Steps for Data Strategy</h2>
        
        <div class="feature-list">
          <div class="feature-item">
            <span class="feature-icon">🛡️</span>
            <div>
              <strong>Consent Mode V2 Setup</strong>
              <p>Run Google tags based on user consent and compensate for data loss with modeling.</p>
            </div>
          </div>
          <div class="feature-item">
            <span class="feature-icon">☁️</span>
            <div>
              <strong>Server-Side Tracking</strong>
              <p>Bypass browser blocks by securely transmitting data from your server to platforms.</p>
            </div>
          </div>
          <div class="feature-item">
            <span class="feature-icon">🎁</span>
            <div>
              <strong>Value Exchange</strong>
              <p>Ask users for their contact info in exchange for a discount, exclusive content, or loyalty program.</p>
            </div>
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="highlight-box">
          <h4>📉 Statistic</h4>
          <p>According to research by Boston Consulting Group (BCG), companies that build their data strategy on first-party data achieve a <strong>1.5x higher return on ad spend (ROI)</strong> and a <strong>2x increase in revenue</strong>.</p>
        </div>

        <h2>🚀 Conclusion</h2>
        <p>The death of 3rd-party data is not a crisis, but an opportunity to place marketing on a more honest and solid foundation. Set up a CRM immediately and start accumulating your user data (with permission).</p>
