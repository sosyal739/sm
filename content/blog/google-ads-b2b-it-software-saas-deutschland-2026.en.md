<p>Many international and domestic technology companies spend tens of thousands of Euros monthly on Google Ads with lackluster deal velocity. The failure almost always stems from three structural mistakes:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Optimizing for Soft Micro-Conversions:</strong> Training Google's bidding algorithm on free whitepapers or generic newsletter signups floods your CRM with students, job seekers, and competitor researchers.</li>
