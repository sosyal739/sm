          <p>Google Ads Performance Max (PMax) is an AI-powered campaign type that allows you to access all of Google's inventory from a single campaign.         <h2>🎯 What is PMax?</h2>
        <p>Performance Max uses Google's machine learning power to automatically serve your ads across YouTube, Display, Search, Discover, Gmail, and Maps.</p>

        <blockquote>
          <p>"Success in PMax isn't about handing over control to Google; it's about feeding Google's AI with the right data."</p>
        </blockquote>

        <h2>💡 Why is Budget Wasted in PMax Campaigns?</h2>
        
        <h3>1. Weak Asset Groups</h3>
        <p>Using insufficient or low-quality images, videos, and text prolongs the algorithm's learning phase and increases costs.</p>

        <h3>2. Wrong Audience Signals</h3>
        <p>If you don't clearly tell Google who your ideal customer is (using 1st-party data, custom segments), your budget will be wasted on irrelevant audiences.</p>

        <h3>3. Lack of Negative Keywords</h3>
        <p>PMax doesn't natively support negative keywords. If you don't exclude your brand name and irrelevant terms at the account level or through your rep, your budget will bleed.</p>
