<h2>⚠️ The Performance Max Black Box: Why Default Setups Drain Margins</h2>
<p>Performance Max consolidates Google's entire ad network (Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Maps, Discover) into an opaque bidding system. The fundamental risk: algorithms seek the path of least resistance. Without brand exclusions, PMax buys users who were already searching for your exact brand name — artificially inflating reported ROAS while delivering zero incremental value.</p>

<h2>🚀 The 7 Core Tenets of High-Profit Performance Max in 2026</h2>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Brand Exclusions:</strong> Build a custom Brand List in account settings and apply it to PMax, ensuring dedicated Search campaigns handle navigation intent efficiently.</li>
  <li><strong>Margin-Tiered Feed Segmentation:</strong> Partition your Google Merchant Center feed into High-Margin (aggressive tROAS targets) and Clearance/Low-Margin product groups.</li>
  <li><strong>First-Party Audience Signals:</strong> Inject high-value Customer Match lists via server-side GTM rather than relying on generic in-market segments.</li>
  <li><strong>Bespoke High-Res Video Assets:</strong> Supply custom vertical and landscape video to prevent Google from auto-generating low-quality slideshows.</li>
  <li><strong>Enhanced Conversions & Consent Mode v2:</strong> Maintain pristine conversion telemetry to feed machine learning bidding engines clean data.</li>
  <li><strong>New Customer Acquisition (NCA) Bidding:</strong> Activate "Bid only for new customers" mode to ensure capital strictly funds net-new market penetration.</li>
  <li><strong>Granular Search Theme Audits:</strong> Continually evaluate PMax search category insights and request manual backend exclusions for irrelevant search intent.</li>
</ol>
