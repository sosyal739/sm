<h2>🔥 Which Platform Fits Your E-Commerce Business Model?</h2>

<h3>1. When to Prioritize Meta Advantage+ Shopping:</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Your Average Order Value (AOV) is above €75:</strong> High-consideration products require Meta's advanced algorithmic retargeting.</li>
  <li><strong>Premium & B2B Products:</strong> High-end fashion, luxury accessories, ergonomic furniture, and health supplements.</li>
  <li><strong>Predictable Multi-Market Scaling:</strong> When scaling budgets from €1,000 to €25,000+ per month with minimal performance volatility.</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. When TikTok Shop Outperforms:</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Products Priced Between €20 and €60:</strong> Low-friction impulse purchases in beauty, gadgets, and trendy apparel.</li>
  <li><strong>Visual Problem Solvers:</strong> Products where the transformation or benefit is immediately obvious within 3 seconds of video.</li>
  <li><strong>UGC & Affiliate Scalability:</strong> Brands ready to collaborate with creator networks on commission-based TikTok affiliate setups.</li>
</ul>

<h2>🚀 The 2026 Hybrid Growth Funnel</h2>
<p>Top-tier e-commerce brands do not treat these channels as competitors; they combine them into a unified growth engine:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Top-of-Funnel (TikTok):</strong> Drive mass low-CPM discovery and impulse sales through short-form video hooks.</li>
  <li><strong>Retargeting & Conversion (Meta Advantage+):</strong> Capture engaged visitors across Instagram and Facebook with dynamic catalog ads.</li>
  <li><strong>Outcome:</strong> Lower overall blended Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) with significantly higher Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER).</li>
</ol>
