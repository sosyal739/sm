<h2>🔄 Why Single-Channel Ad Strategies Fail in 2026</h2>
<p>Pouring your entire marketing budget into just one ad network leaves massive revenue on the table:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Google Ads Only:</strong> You only reach people who already know what to search for. Once search volume is saturated, your Cost Per Click (CPC) skyrockets and revenue plateaus.</li>
  <li><strong>Meta Ads Only:</strong> Users view your engaging video ads on mobile but often do not buy immediately. When they later Google your brand name or product, a competitor running Google Search Ads can easily hijack that sale.</li>
  <li><strong>The Hybrid Synergy Model (Salih Maral Strategy):</strong> Meta Ads builds awareness and creates demand; Google Ads secures that intent and converts it into cash flow.</li>
</ul>
